01 / Profile

Biology, through
a physicist’s lens.

Computational biophysicist developing physics-based and machine-learning methods to investigate complex biological systems across molecular and cellular scales.

My research integrates statistical and computational physics, molecular modelling, topological methods, and graph neural networks to study problems ranging from biomolecular interactions and DNA nanotechnology to collective organization in epithelial tissues.

Project 2: Higher order graphs and topological features for biomolecules interaction

  • Developed a topology-aware graph neural network for protein–nucleic acid interaction modeling using complementary atom-, edge-, and triangle-level molecular representations.

PhD Researcher

National University of Singapore (NUS), Singapore

Supervisor: Assoc Prof. Wang Zhisong

Project: Computational Method of DNA Nanomotor

  • Conducted multiscale computational modeling on the nanomechanics and molecular mechanisms of DNA nanomotors and DNA nanotechnology systems to improve the motor’s directional bias on DNA origami.

  • Contributed to four peer-reviewed publications during doctoral research, including two first-author papers.
